About the organisation

Started by a bunch of old schoolmates.

An online creative community where artists respond to a shared theme through their own lens — be it art, dance, music, poetry, photography, or animation. Started by a bunch of old schoolmates with diverse skills, it quickly grew into something bigger: a platform for expression, collaboration, and creativity.

Why we exist

One idea, infinite interpretations

A blank page is intimidating. A shared theme, on the other hand, becomes a meeting point — a dancer, a painter, a photographer and a poet can all start from the exact same idea and arrive somewhere completely different. That difference is the whole point of the Journal: artists from all backgrounds sharing their unique take, visual, written, or performative, until what we get is a gallery of voices, united by a single spark.

What we believe
  • No artform is more "serious" than another — a plate of food can answer a theme as fully as a painting can.
  • Interpretation is personal. We set the theme; the meaning is entirely up to the artist.
  • Everyone is credited, always, for exactly what they made.
How it's run

Volunteer-organised, once a year

The Journal is not a company or a studio — it's a small organising team who volunteer their time each year to set the theme, open the call, collect submissions, and put the finished collection together.

Set the theme

The team lands on one theme a year, chosen to be open enough for any artform.

Run the open call

Anyone can apply — no portfolio requirements, no entry fee, no single "right" medium.

Publish the collection

Every submission is catalogued here and shared on Instagram, credited to its artist.
