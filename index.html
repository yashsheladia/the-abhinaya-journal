The idea in short

Abhinaya means expression.
The Journal is where it's kept.

In Indian classical dance, abhinaya is the art of expression — the gesture, the face, the feeling that carries a story past words. Once a year, we ask a wide circle of artists to bring that same expressive instinct to a single shared theme, in whatever medium is theirs.

Step 01

A theme is chosen

Each year we set one open, evocative theme — broad enough for any artform to answer.

Step 02

Artists respond, freely

Paint it, dance it, photograph it, cook it, write it, sing it — any medium is welcome.

Step 03

The Journal is published

Every submission is collected, credited, and shared here and on Instagram as one collective body of work.
