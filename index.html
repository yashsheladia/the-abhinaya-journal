The idea in short

Abhinaya (अभिनय) means expression in Sanskrit.
That's what we celebrate.

One idea, infinite interpretations. Once a year, we ask a wide circle of artists to bring their own take — visual, written, or performative — to a single shared theme. What we get back is a gallery of voices, united by a single spark.

Step 01

A theme is chosen

Each year we set one open, evocative theme — broad enough for any artform to answer.

Step 02

Artists respond, freely

Paint it, dance it, photograph it, cook it, write it, sing it — any medium is welcome.

Step 03

The Journal is published

Every submission is collected, credited, and shared here and on Instagram as one collective body of work.
