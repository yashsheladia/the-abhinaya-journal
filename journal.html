The concept

An online creative community, built around a shared theme.

Artists respond to a shared theme through their own lens — be it art, dance, music, poetry, photography, or animation. Started by a bunch of old schoolmates with diverse skills, the Journal quickly grew into something bigger: a platform for expression, collaboration, and creativity.

Why "Abhinaya?"

Abhinaya (अभिनय) means expression in Sanskrit

And that's what we celebrate — one idea, infinite interpretations. A journal is usually one person's private record; ours is the opposite, a shared one, written collectively by everyone who takes part.

How it works

One spark, a gallery of voices

Artists from all backgrounds share their unique take on the theme — visual, written, or performative. What we get is a gallery of voices, united by a single spark.

How to take part

Four steps, once a year

The open call runs on roughly the same rhythm every edition. Exact dates for the current theme are always posted on Instagram first.

1

The theme is announced

Posted on Instagram and here, with a short note on how loosely or tightly to interpret it.

2

You make your piece

This year: pick a song, and let it shape a piece of art in whatever medium you already work in.

3

You submit it

Send us the piece plus a line on the song you chose and why. Submission details go out with the theme announcement.

4

It joins the Journal

Credited to you, published on the Projects page and on Instagram, alongside everyone else's response.

A few common questions

Good to know

Do I need to be a professional artist?

No. The open call is exactly that — open. Hobbyists, students, and first-timers take part alongside trained artists every year.

Can I submit more than one piece?

Usually, yes, within reason — check the specific submission guidelines posted with each year's theme announcement.

Is there a fee to enter?

No. Taking part in the Journal is free.

Who owns my work afterwards?

You do. We publish and credit your piece as part of that year's collection; you keep every right to it.
